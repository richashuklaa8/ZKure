# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive technical feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal or medical information.
- Credit contributors and respect licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the repository maintainers privately through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as possible.
